# xxchun Connect

xxchun Connect is a fork of **SuperGPT Connect** (https://github.com/superpilot69/supergpt-connect, MIT, © its authors),
made for the xxchun shop (https://xxchun.com). SuperGPT Connect's configuration engine is trimmed from **CC Switch**
(MIT, © Jason Young); see `UPSTREAM.md` and `licenses/`, which are kept as they were.

What this fork changes is listed in `XXCHUN-CHANGES.md`. The MIT licence in `LICENSE` continues to apply to the whole.
